Lemon Medical Aesthetic Clinic in District 22 and Chitgar

Lemon Medical Aesthetic Clinic In West Tehran, near Chitgar Lake, it offers a full range of aesthetic services under one roof: from filler and Botox injections and laser hair removal with Candela devices to rejuvenation, facials and specialist skin treatment. All treatments are performed by Lemon's physicians, and we inject filler without diluting it with normal saline.

Fillers & Gels

Lip, cheek and under-eye fillers plus full-face contouring, with authentic filler, never diluted with normal saline, which lasts until the end of the brand's cycle (6 months to 2 years).

FAQ

Where is Lemon Aesthetic Clinic?

Lemon is located in West Tehran, District 22, near Chitgar Lake, on Kharazi Highway, Ardestani Boulevard, Parseh Commercial Complex, 8th Floor. Access from the main highways of West Tehran is easy.

What services does Lemon offer?

Fillers and Botox, laser hair removal with Candela devices, facial rejuvenation and lifting, mesogel, facial threads, facials, skin treatment (acne, pigmentation and scars) and more — all under one roof.

Is Lemon's filler authentic?

Yes. We inject filler without diluting it with normal saline, which means you receive the exact volume you pay for, and the filler lasts until the end of the brand's real cycle (6 months to 2 years).

Who performs the treatments?

Lemon's physicians perform the injections and treatments in a sterile environment, focused on natural results.

Can I see the result on my own photo before the injection?

Yes. With Lemon Smart Mirror you can see the approximate filler and contour result on your own face before the injection. It is free.

How do I book an appointment at Lemon Clinic?

You can fill in the form on the contact and booking page to continue on WhatsApp, or call the clinic directly.
