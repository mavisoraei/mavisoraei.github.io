What is AI face analysis, and what do you get?

AI face analysis means you upload one simple photo of your face and Lemon’s Smart Mirror analyses it for free within seconds. You get two things: first, a precise facial beauty score out of 100 based on five symmetry and proportion criteria, and second, a visual simulation of the approximate result of lip filler or contouring on your own photo. Neither is a medical diagnosis; their job is to give you a clearer picture and more realistic expectations before you make any decision.

People search for this tool under many different names: face analysis, facial analysis, facial beauty analysis, face assessment, facial beauty test with a photo, or AI facial beauty test. They are all the same thing and all lead to this page, so you do not need to hunt for the right name.

How It Works

  1. Upload a photo:You upload one simple photo of your face.
  2. The AI analyzes:The proportions and symmetry of your facial features are assessed.
  3. See the score:The facial beauty test score out of 100 appears on screen. Up to this point you need neither a sign-up nor a phone number.
  4. Verify your number:For the photo step, a text message code arrives and you enter it.
  5. See your result:Treatment simulation on your own face, before any decision.

Facial Beauty Test Without a Photo

A facial beauty test without a photo is not possible, because the score is calculated from that very photo of yours. But what people usually worry about, namely signing up and giving a phone number, is not needed at this stage: you upload the photo and the score with the five-criteria analysis appears right there, with no user account and no phone number. The number is only needed one step later, when you want the simulated photo of filler or contouring on your own face.

What happens to the photo is clear too: it is stored on Lemon’s own server for at most 6 months. After that the original photo and all the simulated images are deleted automatically. The analysis itself is done by an AI provider whose servers are outside Iran; the photo is sent only for this purpose, without your name or number. The details are on thePrivacy Policyis written.

And if you still prefer not to upload a photo of your face, there is another option right here: the “Talk to Lemon’s Smart Assistant” button at the top of this page. You ask your question by voice and get the answer on the spot, without uploading anything. The fees are also on thePriceand real examples inGalleryare available without any upload.

Facial Beauty Test: How the Score Out of 100 Is Calculated

The score is not guesswork. The Smart Mirror measures five specific criteria on your own photo precisely and gives each one a number out of 100:

  • Overall Facial Symmetry
  • Eye Proportion
  • Nose Proportion
  • Lip Proportion
  • Chin and Jaw Proportion

The final score is the average of those five numbers, along with a short sentence and an estimate of apparent age. Because the criteria are fixed, once you see the filler or contouring simulation you can place the before and after side by side and see which criterion shifted.

One point that makes a real difference: the number is not random. Upload the same photo again and you get the same score. The measurement is based on the criteria above, not on a draw.

You'll also be given the ideal range for this number:This is a beauty assessment, not a medical diagnosis and not a verdict about anyone. No treatment decision should be made on this score alone; that decision belongs to a medical consultation.

FAQ

Is the Smart Mirror free?
Yes, it costs nothing. Face analysis and the facial beauty test are done with no sign-up and without giving a phone number. To generate the simulation photo, you verify your number with a text code; that is free too.
Do I have to give my number to see the score?
No. The facial beauty score and the five-criteria analysis come with no number and no sign-up. The number is only needed for the next step, when you want the simulated photo of filler or contouring on your own face.
How does it work?
You upload a photo of your face, the AI analyses it, shows the facial beauty score out of 100 and puts the approximate result of treatments such as lip filler and contouring onto your own face.
What is the AI facial beauty test?
The AI measures five criteria on your own photo precisely: overall symmetry, eye proportion, nose proportion, lip proportion, and chin and jaw proportion. It gives each a number out of 100, and the final score is the average of those five. The number is not random and is fixed for a given photo. It is a beauty assessment, not a medical diagnosis.
Are face analysis and facial analysis the same thing?
Yes, they are the same. People write it in different ways: face analysis, facial analysis, facial beauty analysis or face assessment. They all lead to the same tool.
What does a full face analysis include?
The facial beauty score out of 100, plus a visual simulation of the approximate result of lip filler, under-eye filler and full-face contouring on your own photo. There is no medical diagnosis and no prescription in it.
Is the simulation result exactly what I will get?
The simulation is a smart estimate for a better picture. The real result depends on your anatomy and the physician’s opinion and is settled at the consultation.
What form do I receive the result in?
It is a simulated image of your own face showing the approximate result of lip filler or contouring; you see it right there on the page and you can show it to the physician during the consultation as well.
