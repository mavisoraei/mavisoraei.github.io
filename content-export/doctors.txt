doctors-50 | h2 | Verify the medical license number yourself
doctors-51 | p | the medical license number of every physician registered with the Iranian Medical Council is written on their own page and can be verified through the official system of the Medical Council (Dr. Ghafouri is a dental graduate of India and his credentials are on his own page); the verification link is on that same page. This is the only way to fact-check a claim of being a "doctor" from the outside, and we suggest you do the same for any clinic you are considering, not just for Lemon. Each physician's cases are also listed separately on the Case Gallery You can see it by filtering on the doctor's name.
