laser-248 | p | With correct shaving and the right device, 6 to 8 sessions a month apart are usually enough. Incomplete shaving before a session can needlessly extend the course.
laser-249 | summary | Does laser harm the body?
laser-250 | p | No. The laser wavelength penetrates only as deep as the hair follicle and does no damage to underlying tissue, internal organs or the immune system.
laser-251 | summary | Is laser suitable for dark Iranian skin?
laser-252 | p | Yes. For fair to dark skin, the Nd:YAG (1064 nm) and diode (808 nm) wavelengths are safe and effective, and Lemon has both; the physician chooses based on skin tone. Most visitors see results from the first session.

laser-255 | summary | How can I tell the clinic’s laser device really is Candela?
laser-256 | p | Ask two things and look. First, the physician or operator should name the exact device model; at Lemon, GentleMax Pro and GentleLase Pro from Candela’s Gentle Pro series. laser-259 | summary | Can peach fuzz be lasered?

laser-261 | summary | Does beard line laser for men ruin the beard shape?
laser-262 | p | No. Before starting, the physician marks the exact beard line on the skin with a special pencil and only hair outside that line is removed; the beard shape stays untouched.
