Laser Hair Removal

Laser hair removal is an effective and low-maintenance way to permanently reduce unwanted body and facial hair. The laser beam targets the pigment in the hair, its heat weakens the follicle and the hair is gradually eliminated. Unlike electrolysis, which only pulls the hair out from the root and lets it grow back, it is precisely this targeting of the follicle that makes the laser result long-lasting; the full difference between the two methods is on theLaser or Electrolysiswe have explained.

Lemon's advantages:We have all three main laser wavelengths (Alexandrite, diode and Nd:YAG): two platformsCandela GentleMax Pro and GentleLase Proalong with aplus a separate 808 nm diode laser. The physician chooses the appropriate wavelength based on your skin tone and hair type. Most clinics only have one wavelength. The first session always begins with a physician consultation.

Roller or shot; why it matters

Laser devices come in two types: roller and shot (stamped) handpieces. With roller handpieces the tip is shared between clients, and that shared tip carries the hygienic risk of transferring infection (such as HPV) between people.

the same point applies in the form of adedicated hygiene packis prepared for every visit:your own laser handpiece, come with a marking pencil and protective eyewear, all in separate packaging and for you alone; nothing is shared between clients.

Which wavelength for Iranian skin?

There are three main wavelengths in hair removal lasers. Alexandrite (755 nm) is designed for fair European skin. Diode (808 nm) is a good choice for fair-to-medium Iranian skin and relatively dark hair. Nd:YAG (1064 nm) is used for very dark skin. Lemon Clinic has all three wavelengths, and the physician picks the right one at the first session based on your skin tone and hair; having all three options is what separates a good result from an average one. The full comparison of Alexandrite versus diode, and which suits which skin and hair type, is on theAlexandrite or Diode Laser: Which Is Better?we have written.

Which Candela devices does Lemon use?

Lemon Clinic has both the GentleMax Pro and GentleLase Pro platforms from Candela’s Gentle Pro series, along with a separate 808 nm diode laser. Having several devices means the physician matches the device and wavelength to your skin tone and hair type instead of fitting your skin to the only wavelength available; that choice is the difference between an even result and a patchy one.

The Gentle Pro series platforms cover the Alexandrite (755 nm) and Nd:YAG (1064 nm) wavelengths; Alexandrite suits lighter skin and Nd:YAG suits darker skin. The diode laser adds the 808 nm wavelength, which strikes a good balance between efficacy and safety for fair skin and dark hair typical of Iranian skin.

Which device and wavelength are used in your session is the physician’s decision, made at that first consultation based on skin tone, hair thickness and the treatment area.

Candela Hair Removal Laser in West Tehran

“Candela” is a brand, not a device; so if you are looking for Candela laser in West Tehran, what you should ask before booking is the exact model, not just the brand name. Lemon works in West Tehran with the 2025 Candela and we have written its models up above. The next question is how to know that the device over your head during the session really is that one.

Laser is the most requested service from the neighbourhoods around here. For access routes, parking and metro stations see theChitgar Aesthetic Clinicand for the rest of the neighborhoods inWest Tehran aesthetic clinicwe have written.

There is one sign you can feel during your own session. Candela’s Gentle Pro series platforms have DCD cooling: a fraction of a second before each pulse a cryogen spray hits the skin, you hear a short hiss and the skin is briefly cooled. If your session is done on one of these platforms and you did not feel this, ask what the device is. The 808 nm diode laser has contact cooling instead of a spray, so the absence of a spray in a diode session is normal and not a sign of a fault.

Correct shaving; why some people never finish their sessions

When surface hair is long, all the laser energy is absorbed at the surface and never reaches the follicle. But if you shaved with a razor 24 to 48 hours before the session, all the energy goes straight to the follicle. The difference between someone who gets a complete result in 6 to 8 sessions and someone who goes session after session without a result is often this one point.

Fine and unpigmented hair

Laser needs hair pigment; exactly like a magnifying glass that works quickly on a dark surface but not on a light one. White or blond hair without pigment does not respond. Peach fuzz should not be lasered either; the laser first pulls it out of the sleeping phase, thickens it and then eliminates it, which is unpleasant. These areas are identified during the consultation.

Men's Hair Removal Laser

Lemon Clinic is unisex and laser hair removal for men is done with the same devices and the same precision. The areas men ask for most are the face and beard line, neck, chest, abdomen, back and shoulders. The only difference is that men’s hair is usually thicker and denser, so the physician tunes the device power individually for each person based on the actual hair thickness rather than using one fixed setting for everyone.

For the face and beard line, boundary precision matters more than anywhere else on the body; before starting, the physician marks the exact beard line with a special pencil so the beard shape stays untouched and only the hair around it is reduced.

Number of Sessions and Pain

Usually 6 to 8 sessions a month apart are enough. There is no difference for women and men; the physician sets the device level individually for each person. If the hair growth is hormonal or related to polycystic ovaries, more sessions and more regular maintenance are usually needed; in the articleHormonal Hair Laserwe explain why. It is not significantly painful; most visitors describe a warm rubber band snap, and the device’s strong cooling system reduces that sensation while energy is delivered. Numbing is possible on request, but is generally not needed.

Care Before and After Laser

ago:Shave with a razor 24 to 48 hours before the session. Waxing, threading and electrolysis are forbidden because they pull the hair root out. Use only a razor throughout the course.

After:Avoid the sauna, swimming pool and heavy exercise for 48 hours. SPF50 sunscreen is mandatory for two weeks. Mild skin redness for a few days is normal.

If the skin reaction goes beyond a passing redness and the area is burned or blistered, even if you had the laser done elsewhere, see theLaser Burn Treatmentpage; there we explain what causes it and how the cause is identified during an examination.

Contraindications:Pregnancy and breastfeeding. Photosensitising medication (retinoids, tetracycline). Laser cannot be used over tattoos. Dark moles must be avoided.

Starting a Course in the Cold Season

If you want to finish the course before summer, the cold season is a more sensible starting point: there is less sun exposure and the risk of pigmentation after each session is lower. Calling the clinic is enough to book your first session. Exactly when the course finishes, what to do with post-holiday tanned skin, and whether summer laser is possible are all covered inThe Best Season for Hair Removal Laserwe have written.

FAQ

How many laser hair removal sessions are needed?

With correct shaving and the right device, 6 to 8 sessions a month apart are usually enough. Incomplete shaving before a session can needlessly extend the course.

Does laser harm the body?

No. The laser wavelength penetrates only as deep as the hair follicle and does no damage to underlying tissue, internal organs or the immune system.

Is laser suitable for dark Iranian skin?

Yes. For fair to dark skin, the Nd:YAG (1064 nm) and diode (808 nm) wavelengths are safe and effective, and Lemon has both; the physician chooses based on skin tone. Most visitors see results from the first session.

Is laser painful?

It is not significantly painful; most visitors describe a warm rubber band snap. The Candela device’s strong cooling system cools the area while the laser energy is delivered and reduces that sensation. Numbing is possible on request for particular areas.

How can I tell the clinic’s laser device really is Candela?

Ask two things and look. First, the physician or operator should name the exact device model; at Lemon, GentleMax Pro and GentleLase Pro from Candela’s Gentle Pro series. Second, these platforms have DCD cooling: a fraction of a second before each pulse a cryogen spray hits the skin and you hear a short hiss and feel a moment of cooling. The 808 nm diode laser has contact cooling and no such spray, so the absence of a spray in a diode session is normal.

What care is needed after laser?

Avoid the sauna, swimming pool and heavy exercise for 48 hours. SPF50 sunscreen is mandatory for two weeks. Mild redness for a few days is normal.

Can peach fuzz be lasered?

No. Laser relies on hair pigment. Peach fuzz has no pigment, and the laser first pulls it out of the sleeping phase and thickens it. These areas are identified during the consultation and excluded from laser.

Does beard line laser for men ruin the beard shape?

No. Before starting, the physician marks the exact beard line on the skin with a special pencil and only hair outside that line is removed; the beard shape stays untouched.
