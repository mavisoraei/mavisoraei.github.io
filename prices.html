FAQ

How much is Botox at Lemon?

MecPure or Diston Botox is 1,400,000 for the full dose, not per unit. Cat-eye Botox is 1,800,000 and sweat Botox 2,500,000 per area. The number of doses and the area needed depend on your face and are determined at the consultation.

How much do lip fillers cost at Lemon?

Lip filler prices depend on the brand, from 7,000,000 per cc (ATP) up to 28,000,000 (Dermal Style Smile). All fillers are authentic and injected without dilution with normal saline, so they last until the brand's real durability cycle ends.

How much does laser hair removal cost per session?

Laser prices depend on the area and start at 100,000 for between the brows; a full face is 350,000 and the women's total body package is 2,500,000. Laser is performed with several platforms from the Gentle Pro series and an 808-nanometer diode laser (suited to Iranian olive skin).

How much does a thread lift cost?

A standard MediLift thread is 16,000,000, a collagen thread 24,800,000 and a Kag thread, the strongest type, is 34,500,000. The type of thread suited to your face is chosen at the consultation with Dr. Hoosavi.

If I'm not happy with my filler, how much does dissolving it cost?

Lemon's hyaluronic acid fillers are reversible. Injecting the hyaluronidase enzyme to dissolve filler is 3,800,000 per session, under medical supervision. This reversibility is one of the important reasons to choose hyaluronic filler over permanent gel.

Can the prices on this page be paid in instalments?

Yes, and the instalment price is exactly the same cash price as on this page; you do not pay a single rial more, and the clinic itself covers the payment app's fee. No cheque, no deposit and no guarantor are needed, because the app runs the credit check on your own bank account. Lemon has direct agreements with DigiPay and SnappPay, and is also a contracting party for DoctorPay and BeautyPul. Details are on the instalment gel injection page.

Some other specialist services are not on this list; call for their fees and appointments.
