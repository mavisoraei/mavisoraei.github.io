FAQ

How much is Botox at Lemon?

MecPure or Diston Botox is $140 for the full dose, not per unit. Cat-eye Botox is $180 and sweat Botox $250 per area. The number of doses and the area needed depend on your face and are determined at the consultation.

How much do lip fillers cost at Lemon?

Lip filler prices depend on the brand, from $700 per cc (ATP) up to $2,800 (Dermal Style Smile). All fillers are authentic and injected without dilution with normal saline, so they last until the brand's real durability cycle ends.

How much does laser hair removal cost per session?

Laser prices depend on the area and start at $10 for between the brows; a full face is $35 and the women's total body package is $250. Laser is performed with several platforms from the Gentle Pro series and an 808-nanometer diode laser (suited to olive skin).

How much does a thread lift cost?

A standard MediLift thread is $1,600, a collagen thread $2,480 and a Kag thread, the strongest type, is $3,450. The type of thread suited to your face is chosen at the consultation with Dr. Hoosavi.

If I'm not happy with my filler, how much does dissolving it cost?

Lemon's hyaluronic acid fillers are reversible. Injecting the hyaluronidase enzyme to dissolve filler is $380 per session, under medical supervision. This reversibility is one of the important reasons to choose hyaluronic filler over permanent gel.

How do I pay?

Payment is due on the day of treatment. The clinic accepts cash, card and bank transfer; final pricing is confirmed at consultation.
