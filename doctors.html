who does what

At Lemon the division of labour is clear, and it is the same as written on each doctor's page. The injections, lasers and skin treatments are performed by the clinic's aesthetic physicians, not operators. Surgeries are by Dr. Mehrabouran , the clinic's general surgery specialist and blepharoplasty surgeon. Dr. Gholizadeh Researcher & Science Writer Lemon Magazine and injections and visits are not with them; their name appears under the articles they genuinely reviewed.

Verify the medical license number yourself

the medical license number of every physician registered with the Iranian Medical Council is written on their own page and can be verified through the official system of the Medical Council (Dr. Ghafouri is a dental graduate of India and his credentials are on his own page); the verification link is on that same page. This is the only way to fact-check a claim of being a "doctor" from the outside, and we suggest you do the same for any clinic you are considering, not just for Lemon. Each physician's cases are also listed separately on the Case Gallery You can see it by filtering on the doctor's name.

Education and the magazine: two more things this team does

Besides treatment, two other outputs come from this same team. Lemon Academy runs courses for physicians and clinical staff, and the details are on Training Page page. Lemon Magazine also publishes educational articles, each carrying the author's signature and, where relevant, a reviewer's signature. The rule is simple: the reviewer is always someone other than the author, and their name appears only under something they have genuinely read and approved.

This team is not closed. If you are a physician or medical assistant looking for work at an active clinic, the conditions are written in the aesthetic physician vacancies at Lemon and the roles currently open are listed there.

Each doctor's consulting hours differ

The days and hours each doctor is present are written under their name and are fixed. If you would prefer a particular doctor to do your work, say so when booking so the appointment falls on one of their consulting days.
